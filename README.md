# geartify
## Hi there 👋
Faceless tech unboxings, where I try not only to unbox and showcase the product, but also demonstrate practical use cases, how customers can use it, and provide valuable insights with every video.

## About

I have always been fascinated by technology and how it evolves. Geartify was created to share that interest through carefully filmed, faceless videos that go beyond opening the package. Whenever the product allows it, I demonstrate practical use cases, setup steps, compatibility, accessories, installation, gaming, camera capabilities, or other examples that help viewers understand how the product may fit into everyday use.

The goal is **to show both the design and the practical value of technology** through a calm, structured, and visually focused experience.

Geartify content may include:
- technology unboxings and first looks
- product showcases
- practical use-case demonstrations
- setup and installation videos
- gaming and performance tests
- camera tests
- accessory comparisons
- short-form product videos
- original product photography

## Content Philosophy
### Experience and practical value

A product should not only be shown. Whenever possible, the content should demonstrate how the product can be used. Depending on the product, this may include:
- setup and installation
- fit and compatibility
- controls and physical details
- accessory support
- gaming
- camera tests
- daily-use scenarios
- comparisons between models, finishes, or accessories
 
### Calm visual presentation

Geartify uses a faceless, visually focused production style.

The absence of spoken commentary does not mean the absence of information. Product details, observations, demonstrations, and relevant context may be communicated through:
- product handling
- close-up footage
- practical demonstrations
- structured editing
- on-screen text
- original images
- links and supporting information in the description
 
### Honest experience

Geartify aims to present products accurately and honestly. 

A paid collaboration, gifted product, affiliate relationship, or discount code does not guarantee:
- positive feedback
- a recommendation
- omission of relevant issues
- a particular conclusion
- a minimum number of views
- a minimum number of clicks
- sales or conversions

Personal observations and conclusions reflect the actual experience with the product.

## Sponsorships and Brand Collaborations

Geartify accepts sponsorships selectively. A potential collaboration should be:

- relevant to technology and the Geartify audience
- compatible with the channel’s faceless format
- suitable for practical visual demonstration
- based on a product or service that can be represented responsibly
- supported by clear communication from the brand
- free from misleading, deceptive, or unsupported claims

Geartify may decline a collaboration when the product, company, proposed message, contract terms, timeline, or requested content rights do not fit the channel.

---

## Collaboration Formats

Depending on the campaign, Geartify may offer:

### YouTube integration

A focused product segment placed naturally inside a broader YouTube video. The product must fit the main subject of the video. An integration must not unnecessarily interrupt the pacing or take attention away from the primary product.

### Dedicated YouTube showcase

A complete video focused on one product or a collection of related products. A dedicated showcase may include:

- packaging and unboxing
- design and material details
- setup or installation
- product fitting
- controls, ports, buttons, and physical details
- practical use cases
- compatibility demonstrations
- comparison between models or colors
- final product presentation
 
### Short-form content

Campaign footage may be adapted into short-form content for TikTok and Instagram. A normal dedicated-video workflow may include:

- one short TikTok edit
- one extended TikTok edit
- one short Instagram Reel
- one extended Instagram Reel

Exact durations and deliverables are agreed separately for each campaign.

### Original product photography

Geartify may create original photographs during the production process. Depending on the campaign, this may include:

- an Instagram image post
- an Instagram carousel
- a YouTube Community post
- supporting promotional images linked to the published video
The number of photographs and permitted usage are agreed separately.

## Sponsored Showcase Versus Independent Review

A sponsored product showcase and an independent review are not necessarily the same format.

### Sponsored showcase

A sponsored showcase may focus on:

- unboxing
- design
- product features
- setup
- fit
- compatibility
- agreed practical demonstrations

The sponsorship does not allow the brand to determine Geartify’s personal opinion or require unsupported statements.

### Independent review or evaluation

An independent review may contain broader evaluative observations, criticism, comparisons, preferences, and conclusions. Geartify decides whether the available testing and experience are sufficient to describe content as a review.

A brand may not require a positive review as a condition of payment.

## Brand Product Information and Factual Verification

For sponsored content, a brand may verify factual information before publication. Brands should coordinate internal feedback through a primary campaign contact and provide clear, timestamped comments.

Factual verification does not give a brand control over:

- honest observations
- preferences
- criticism
- conclusions
- editorial opinion
- filming style
- camera presentation
- ASMR format
- pacing
- disclosure wording
- video length within the agreed range

Requests for additional filming, material restructuring, new demonstrations, or additional deliverables may require a separate agreement and fee.

## Gifted Products

Sending a product without a written paid-campaign agreement does not guarantee:

- publication
- a video
- a social-media post
- a link
- a review
- a publication date
- draft approval
- return of the product, unless agreed before shipment

Geartify retains editorial discretion over unsolicited and gifted products. If a gifted product is featured, the relationship will be disclosed where required.

## Disclosure and Transparency

Paid sponsorships, gifted products, affiliate relationships, and other material brand connections are disclosed using the relevant platform tools and visible content disclosures where applicable.

Depending on the relationship, disclosures may include wording such as:

- Paid partnership with [Brand]
- Sponsored by [Brand]
- Product provided by [Brand]
- Affiliate links included

A sponsorship does not remove Geartify’s responsibility to communicate honestly with viewers.

---

## Affiliate Links and Referral Programs

Geartify participates in affiliate and referral programs, including Amazon Associates where applicable. When a viewer purchases through an eligible affiliate link, Geartify may receive a commission. This normally does not increase the price paid by the viewer.

Affiliate links help support:
- future product purchases
- filming equipment
- software and production costs
- continued development of the channel

Affiliate participation does not guarantee positive feedback or inclusion of a product. Products are linked when they are relevant to the content, useful for viewers, or directly connected to the video.

## Content Usage Rights

Unless separately agreed in writing, brands may:
- share links to the original Geartify publication
- use platform-native sharing features that preserve the original publication and attribution

Brands may not automatically:

- download and re-upload Geartify content
- remove Geartify attribution
- edit or create derivative versions
- use the content in paid advertising
- use the content in Spark Ads or Partnership Ads
- publish the content on product pages
- use the content on Amazon or retailer listings
- use original photographs in advertising or marketing
- request raw footage
- sublicense content to agencies, retailers, distributors, or other third parties
- claim perpetual commercial usage rights

Any additional usage must be agreed in writing and should define:

- permitted content
- platforms
- purpose
- territory
- duration
- editing rights
- attribution
- licensing fee

## Campaign Timing

Publication schedules depend on:

- product availability
- tracked delivery
- compatibility
- receipt of the agreed campaign information
- timely factual verification
- the existing Geartify production schedule

A sponsored accessory must not delay a time-sensitive primary launch video unless this has been explicitly agreed in writing. If a product arrives after the filming cutoff for a launch integration, Geartify may propose a dedicated follow-up video or another suitable format.

---

## Payment and Agreements

Guaranteed sponsored deliverables require a written agreement.

The agreement should define:

- exact deliverables
- campaign fee and currency
- products supplied
- publication expectations
- factual-verification process
- content usage rights
- payment terms
- cancellation terms
- disclosure
- responsibilities of both parties

The payment period begins from the event stated in the written agreement, such as invoice receipt, draft approval, or publication. Payment is not conditional upon achieving a minimum number of views, clicks, sales, or conversions unless Geartify explicitly agrees otherwise in writing.
